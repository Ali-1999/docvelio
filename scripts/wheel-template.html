    How to use the random spinning wheel

    Type or paste names in the entry box, one name per line. Press Spin to select an option. You can remove the last winner to avoid repeats, shuffle entries, or increase an option's probability using a weight. The outcome is selected with the browser's cryptographic random number generator; the animation visually displays the chosen result.

    Uses for the wheel

    Use this free wheel to choose students for classroom activities, draw names for informal games, pick tasks for meetings, or decide between everyday options. Results are independently random unless you remove entries or change weights. For regulated prize draws, follow applicable rules and use an independently audited draw process.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the wheel free?

    Yes. This tool runs in your browser without requiring an account.

    Can I save or share a wheel?

    Yes. Settings are saved locally, and you can export a JSON file or copy a share link with your current entries and options.

    Can names have different chances?

    Yes. Add a positive weight after the entry, such as Alex | 2. A weight of two is twice as likely as a weight of one.
