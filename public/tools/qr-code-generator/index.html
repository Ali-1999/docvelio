Frequently asked questions

Are these QR codes free forever?

Yes. These are static QR codes generated entirely in your browser. Docvelio does not host a redirect service for them.

Can I make a QR code for a Wi-Fi password?

Yes. Select Wi-Fi, enter the exact network name and password, then generate and scan with a compatible device.

Does Docvelio upload my QR code content?

No. This generator encodes your content directly in your browser using a locally served JavaScript library. Avoid entering highly sensitive credentials on shared devices.

Can I edit an existing QR code?

You can change its contents here and regenerate it. Once a static QR code has been printed, its encoded content cannot be changed.
