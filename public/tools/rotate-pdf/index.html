Rotate PDF Pages

Turn all pages 90, 180, or 270 degrees and save the result.

Files are processed in your browser. No PDF contents are sent to this website by the tool.

How to rotate pdf pages

  1. Choose a PDF to rotate.
  2. Select 90, 180 or 270 degrees clockwise.
  3. Click Rotate PDF and inspect the saved document.

When this tool is useful

Rotation updates the PDF pages, rather than merely rotating the preview on your screen. This version applies the same rotation to every page. If only one page is sideways, you may prefer to extract that page, correct it, and merge it back with the rest of the document.

Before sharing: Open the output and verify page order, text, images and formatting. This is a straightforward utility, not a replacement for professional document review.

Frequently asked questions

Will text quality be reduced?

Rotation normally changes page orientation metadata without rasterising the page.

Can I rotate only page five?

Not in this starter version. It rotates the entire document.

Processing depends on your device and browser memory. Very large documents may take longer or fail.

Correct scanned documents before sharing

Pages can arrive sideways after scanning receipts, photographing forms or combining files from different sources. Select a rotation that makes the text read correctly, and confirm whether your entire document needs the same correction. This version rotates every page by the selected angle.

Rotation changes how pages are viewed, but it does not run OCR, correct skewed photographs or repair low-resolution scans. If text remains difficult to read, consider rescanning at a suitable resolution. Verify that any signatures and page edges remain visible after export.
