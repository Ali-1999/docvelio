Remove Pages from a PDF

Delete unwanted page ranges and download the cleaned PDF.

Files are processed in your browser. No PDF contents are sent to this website by the tool.

How to remove pages from a pdf

  1. Choose one PDF.
  2. Enter page numbers you want removed, such as 2,5-7.
  3. Click Remove Pages and download the shortened document.

When this tool is useful

Removing pages can make shared documents easier to read, especially when a scan contains blank separators or duplicates. This operation creates a new PDF without the selected pages; do not mistake it for permanent redaction of sensitive content inside pages that remain.

Before sharing: Open the output and verify page order, text, images and formatting. This is a straightforward utility, not a replacement for professional document review.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove every page?

No. At least one page must remain in the exported PDF.

Are my original pages deleted from my device?

No. The process creates a new output and leaves the source PDF unchanged.

Processing depends on your device and browser memory. Very large documents may take longer or fail.

Remove blank scans and duplicate pages

Multipage scans often contain blank separators, repeated pages or outdated inserts. Remove those pages to simplify a document before emailing or printing it. Page numbering starts from the first PDF page, even when the printed document begins on a later number.

Unlike hiding a paragraph with a white box, removing a whole page excludes that page from the new output. However, it does not inspect the remaining pages for private information, comments or attached material. Always review the exported PDF before sharing it with other people.

To keep only selected pages instead, open Split PDF. Our page-organising guide explains a safe review process.
