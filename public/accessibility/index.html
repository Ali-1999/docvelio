Accessibility at Docvelio

We want Docvelio to be usable with keyboards, screen readers and a wide range of screen sizes. The site uses semantic links and buttons, form labels and visible focus states where possible.

Known limitations

PDF canvas previews and drawing tools can be difficult to use without a pointer and may not expose full document semantics to assistive technology. Some imported DOCX content may lose accessibility metadata when converted to editable HTML.

Report a problem

If a feature is difficult to access, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Please tell us which page and browser or assistive technology you are using. We aim to use your report to improve the experience.
