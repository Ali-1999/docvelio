## Page briefs: question headings, related links and constraints
1. **Edit PDF:** 'Can I edit existing PDF text?', 'How do I add a highlight?', 'Does covering text redact it?' → sign, fill, safety guide. Original text replacement and safe redaction must not be claimed.
2. **Merge PDF:** 'How do I merge in the right order?', 'Can I rearrange pages?', 'Will originals change?' → split, compress, rotate. Verify page-level control first.
3. **Compress PDF:** 'Why did my PDF not shrink?', 'Does compression reduce image quality?', 'Can I achieve a precise size?' → merge, image compression, privacy. Avoid exact-size guarantees.
4. **Sign PDF:** 'Can I draw or type my signature?', 'Can I sign on a phone?', 'Is a visual signature the same as a certified digital signature?' → fill, edit, privacy. Do not promise legally binding authentication.
5. **Resize image:** 'How do I enter exact dimensions?', 'How do I keep aspect ratio?', 'What happens when I enlarge?' → crop, convert, reduce KB. Avoid claiming recovered detail.
6. **Crop image:** 'How do I crop to square?', 'What is crop versus resize?', 'Can I preview?' → resize, convert. Confirm export dimensions.
7. **Reduce image KB:** 'Can I reduce to 100 KB?', 'Why can the target fail?', 'How does quality change?' → resize, convert. Show measured output size.
8. **Word editor:** 'Can I edit DOCX without Word?', 'Can tables or layouts change?', 'How do I export?' → edit PDF, privacy. Disclose formatting limitations.
9. **Fill PDF:** 'How do I fill a PDF form?', 'Can I add checks and dates?', 'Why is a field not editable?' → sign, edit. Confirm interactive vs flattened PDF support.
10. **PDF safety:** 'Is covering text with a shape secure?', 'What is real PDF redaction?', 'Are browser files automatically private?' → edit PDF, privacy, contact. Avoid inaccurate absolute security claims.
These headings are proposals, NOT transcribed Google People Also Ask questions.
